# LingoRoom Studio — Manual Test Checklist

1. Sign in as teacher and student in two separate browsers and open the same confirmed lesson.
2. Enter LingoRoom from both accounts; confirm both see the same lesson session.
3. Test mute/unmute and camera on/off from each account.
4. Click Share screen, select a window or screen in the browser prompt, and confirm the other account sees it. Stop sharing and confirm the status badge clears.
5. Raise and lower a hand; test captions and tile layout.
6. Open the full-width whiteboard from the live-room toolbar.
7. Draw with the pen, switch color, use the highlighter, then erase a mark. Confirm both accounts receive the drawing.
8. Confirm the student can add strokes but cannot Undo or Clear. Confirm the teacher can Undo and Clear.
9. Open Textbook and Practice from the Studio tools panel; confirm the PDF page and lesson content still synchronize.
10. Open Class Chat and verify it remains inside LingoRoom only.
11. Test fullscreen and Leave, then re-enter.
12. Repeat on mobile browsers and test the browser's screen-sharing capability; some mobile platforms limit screen capture.

13. In the teacher demo, draw on the local board, change pen colors, open the browser screen picker, and confirm the local preview is visible only to the teacher.
